Rules & caveats

Watch: one trigger. Warning: two triggers. Alert: three triggers. Rules require SPI-3 ≤ −1 for two consecutive months, a VCI fall ≥ 15 points over two months, or root-zone soil moisture at or below the calendar-month 10th percentile. Missing soil observations remain gaps. This is a climate-indicator signal, not a displacement forecast.

Formulas

DPI: weighted rainfall, vegetation, soil-moisture, vegetation-health and heat components; available weights are renormalized.
CDRS: 100 × (0.50H + 0.35E + 0.15V). H = DPI/100; E = highest selected-hazard exposure share; V = min–max scaled vulnerable share.
Classes: Low 0–25, Moderate 25–50, High 50–75, Very high 75–100.
